## Recorded deviations

The engine-difference register from `parity/README.md`: differences the layout gate leaves out, each approved by the root, with negative controls in `parity/test/register.test.ts`.

- **A. SVG paint-server content.** Where: `trends-default` and `trends-weekly`, the `<rect>` inside the chart's `<pattern>` in `<defs>`. `geometry.js` skips SVG `defs`, `pattern`, `clipPath`, `mask`, `symbol`, `marker`, `linearGradient`, `radialGradient` and `filter`, with their descendants. They paint nothing where they sit. An HTML element with one of those names is kept.
- **B. Inline boxes inside an overflowing ellipsis container.** Where: `match-history-narrow-900`, the truncated friend name's `bdi`. `geometry.js` marks a row `"ellipsis"` when an ancestor has `text-overflow: ellipsis`, an `overflow-x` other than `visible`, and `scrollWidth > clientWidth`. When both engines mark a row, the gate compares its x, y and height and not its width, and counts it in `widthExempt`. The container itself is compared in full.
- **Port elements marked `data-parity-skip`.** Not an engine difference but a deliberate one in the port's markup. Where Electron drew one element and the port needs a wrapper to draw the same thing in WebKit, the port marks the outer element `data-parity-skip`, and `geometry.js` records its box and leaves out what it wraps. T27's `ChampionIcon` is the case: Electron draws the portrait as one `img` with `object-view-box`, which WKWebView ignores, and the port draws a clipping span at that box with a larger `img` inside. An unmarked extra element still fails the gate.
- Rule B applied, rows compared without width: macOS `global-stats-default` (3), macOS `match-history-narrow-900` (2), macOS `global-stats-augments` (3), macOS `global-stats-items` (3).
