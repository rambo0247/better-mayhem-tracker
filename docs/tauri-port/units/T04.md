## What could not be captured

The tray icon, the open tray menu, the macOS menu bar, native `<select>` popups and native `title` tooltips are native, and Screen Recording is not granted. The JSON dump and handler probes are the baseline. Which click opens the tray menu (left on macOS, right on Windows) is OS behavior and recorded only as a note. Electron's `double-click` also fires on macOS. Tauri has no macOS double-click event, which is why the handler's effect is recorded.
