## Synthetic state

Through the seam, never the data folder: `getRecoveryReport`, `getUpdateNotes`, `getLaunchInstall` (taken from the real `checkForUpdate` against the fake), `downloadUpdate` held open, `copyCardImage` and `exportCardImage` (refused by default, replaceable by a state, held open for the busy states), and pushed events `update:progress`, `update:install-failed`, `lcu:backfill-progress`, `lcu:backfill-done`. `checkForUpdate` itself runs the real updater code.

`realCall` runs the original handler of a channel, and refuses `exportCardImage` and `copyCardImage` unconditionally. A run can replace what those two answer. It can never call them, so no step writes the operator's clipboard or opens a save dialog.

### The 57 s state

An earlier run (the first at `2eb23d5`, 269 s wall, kept as `parity/out/run1/timing.json`) took 57.3 s on `overlay-recovery-corrupt-restored-unkept`. `overlay-recovery-corrupt-empty-unkept` took 16.9 s, `...empty-kept` 15.4 s, `overlay-toast-importing` 12.5 s and `...corrupt-restored-kept` 11.2 s around it. The verifier's first run (`/tmp/verify-T04/capture-run1.log`) had one 11.9 s state, also `overlay-toast-importing`. What the data shows:

- It is not the state. The same state took 3.6 to 3.7 s in every other run, and no state outside those two runs took over 8 s.
- It is not the page. `settleMs` was 1.56 s in the slow states, the same as the fast ones, so the extra 12 to 55 s was outside `settle`.
- It is not the overlay hooks. The slow states are consecutive and mixed (`getRecoveryReport` overrides and an `emit`), and the states before and after ran at normal speed.
- The window is contiguous and ends by itself. Five consecutive states cost about 112 s more than usual, then the run returned to 3.6 s a state. That fits the machine, which sat at load average 30 to 40 from other lanes' builds, and not the code.

I could not make it recur. Five complete captures at this round's code (one with a per-phase timer, four timed) show no state over 8 s. With the timer, a normal state costs the 1.5 s first settle, the 1.5 s final settle, the 0.3 s mouse park and a screenshot of about 0.5 s. So the stall's cause is inferred, not proven. To prove it the next time, `applyState` now prints `slow <id>: <ms>, reset .., pre steps .., resize and load .., first settle .., card .., steps .., final settle ..` when a state takes over 10 s. Set beside `capture.ts`'s own per-state time, the difference is the geometry dump and the screenshot.

## Fake GitHub download URLs (T24 note)

1.2.1's updater refuses a download URL that does not start with `https://github.com/rambo0247/better-mayhem-tracker/`. The fake hands out `https://github.com/rambo0247/better-mayhem-tracker/releases/download/v<tag>/<name>` in every scenario, and the seam rewrites `github.com/.../releases/download/` as well as the `api.github.com` releases page onto the fake, keeping the path. The asset list includes the x64 dmg.

The download route serves a small fixed payload with its `Content-Length`, and the `digest` on every asset is the sha256 of that payload (`PAYLOAD_DIGEST`), so a lane gets a passing download from the real updater. The dead pieces were removed: the stalled download, `setDefault` and the `port` field. T24 keeps the github.com URL shape, the route and the digest rule. T24 still has to serve the real Tauri zip or exe under the real asset names, and add a wrong-digest variant for its lane 6 (design note `_design/T24-how.md`).
