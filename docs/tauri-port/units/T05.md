## Per-state recording

`bun parity/record-states.ts` (run after `record-ipc.ts`) runs all 63 states of `states.ts` in trunk Electron with the seam recording every renderer invoke. It writes `states/<id>.json` (each call's arguments, method and the case that answers it), recorded 5,218 calls on the final run and adds 239 `state-<hash>` cases where no case had those arguments and that outcome. 155 of them are `resolveAugmentIcon`, since `renderer-calls.ts` came from T07's runs on the earlier fixture. A call is matched to a case by arguments and outcome together (T07 found `getMatchFilterOptions()` pointed at `queues-hidden` where the page got `default`). `test/record-states.test.ts` covers that and the two checks below.

Before each state, the recorder waits until `getLcuStatus` answers a settled status, the connect loop's first tick. The three states that hold the loop (`status-connecting`, `live-standby-connecting`, `match-history-empty-connecting`) keep `"connecting"` by design. Against the goldens recorded before the merge, 8 states' `getLcuStatus` answers changed: `match-history-default` and `match-history-empty-waiting` from `connecting` to `disconnected`; `challenges-offline-empty`, `live-standby-offline-empty`, `match-history-empty-offline` and `status-disconnected` from `connecting, disconnected` to `disconnected`; `live-standby-connected` from `connecting, connected` to `connected`; `match-history-filter-menu` is new (T02's open filter menu) and answers `disconnected`.

The recorder fails when a row that seeds `view:` keys makes the same page calls as the default state on its route. All six match-history rows differ from `match-history-default` in their `getMatchHistory` arguments: `sort: "score"` (sorted-score), `sortDir: "asc"` (oldest), both (sorted-score-ascending), `favorites: true` (favorites, also `getMatchSessions`), `multikills: ["doubles"]` (multikills-pressed, also `getMatchSessions`), and `favorites: true, multikills: ["pentas"]` (no-matches, also `getMatchSessions`). In the goldens from before the merge all six equalled the default. `trends-weekly` is exempt: Trends buckets the same daily rows by week or month, so its calls equal `trends-default`'s by design. The state's own `expect` (the Weekly button pressed) proves the key applied.

It also fails when any of the five reads `resolveVars` makes would replay by arguments to a different answer than Electron gave. Six states take a route variable from those reads: `game-card-default` (`gameId` 5684865505, `getGameCard([5684865505])`), `season-card-default` (`seasonId` `all`), `season-card-numbered` (`set-3`), `friend-detail-default` (`friendKey`), `group-detail-default` (`groupKey`) and `global-champion-detail-default` (`championId` 26). Each read now picks exactly one case by arguments (`newest`, and `default` for the other four), holding Electron's answer, so the Tauri replay resolves the same ids. Across the whole golden set one argument list still ties at the lowest `seq`: `getGameRecap([7000000000])`, made only in client states, whose lists name the case.

`backfill-toast-done` and `backfill-toast-error` timed out on their toast text in all four recordings this round and carry an `error` field with the calls made until then. T04's overlays states are not in this base.

## Waivers and notes

- Goldens for `export.json` and the card PNGs stay uncommitted, per the root. Nothing under `parity/out/` is committed.
- The merge of T02's `9a3802a` kept every T02 guard and T05's seam hooks. In `seam.cjs` T03's process-scan block, SGP redirect and fake-client connect filter stay, and T02's `isLoopback` replaces the older loopback pattern in all of them. T02's live-port guard and `PARTIAL_RASTER_OFF` are in.
- T03's fake client fails about 1 launch in 256: `fake-lcu/ca.ts` writes a random 16-byte serial as a DER integer without dropping a leading zero byte. When the CA's serial starts `00` and the next byte is below `80`, the encoding is non-minimal, Node drops the CA, and every request fails with `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY_LEAF_SIGNATURE` (seen once this round, `backfill-running: the app never subscribed`). A forced serial reproduces that error, and a leaf with such a serial fails the fake's own server start. The fix is in T03's file, so it goes to the root.
- The `update:*` events come from the fake GitHub plus an `isPackaged` patch. The download fails its digest and nothing spawns.
- The first two timing and launch attempts ran under `taskpolicy -b` and failed on timeouts. Launches now use `capture-slot.sh`, as the rules say.
